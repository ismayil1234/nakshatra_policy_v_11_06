Your Order Cancellation Rights

You are entitled to cancel Your Order within 7 days without giving any reason for doing so.

The deadline for cancelling an Order is 7 days from the date on which You place the order.

In order to exercise Your right of cancellation, You must inform Us of your decision by means of a clear statement. You can inform us of your decision by:

By visiting this page on our website: www.jioragoldanddiamonds.com

By sending us an email: service@jioragoldanddiamonds.com

We will reimburse You no later than 14 days from the day on which We receive the order cancellation request. We will use the same means of payment as You used for the Order, and You will not incur any fees for such reimbursement, but note that the order can be cancelled only before shipping.
